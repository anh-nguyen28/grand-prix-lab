# Team logos

Optional. Add a logo image you are allowed to use, named after the team id, and the game will show it
on the player's car (sidepods and rear wing), in the showroom, the race HUD, the timing tower, the
car nameplate and the results. Without a file, each team shows an original monogram badge in its colours.

Accepted: `<id>.svg` (preferred) or `<id>.png` with a transparent background.

Team ids: mclaren, ferrari, redbull, mercedes, astonmartin, alpine, williams, racingbulls, haas, audi, cadillac
